# Remnants | The Modpack — Season 1: *The Long Eclipse*

The story bible. Quests, item names, NPC lines and the pack page all draw from this file. NightBeam's fights are
called "soulslike"; no other game is ever named.

## The pitch (one breath)

The sun was eaten an age ago. Everything that died in the dark came back wrong, as **Remnants**: echoes that keep
a name, a title and a grudge. You wake in the ash with nothing, one of the **Ashborn**. The only light left lives in
**Ember Shrines**. Relight the world one shrine and one Sovereign at a time, and when you die, your own Remnant will
be waiting where you fell.

## The world before

- **The Sun-Crown.** Long ago the world had one sun and one crown. Seven rulers, the **Sovereigns**, swore to share
  the crown's light, and broke the sun into **Fragments** so each could hold a piece.
- **The Eighth.** One more wanted all of it. He drank the last light himself and became the **Hollow Sovereign**:
  a crown with nothing inside. Where he sits, under a black sun, is **the Eclipsed Realm**.
- **The Long Eclipse.** With the light swallowed, the dead stopped resting. Every creature that dies under the
  Eclipse may rise again stronger: a **Veteran**, then an **Elite**, a **Champion**, and rarest of all a
  **Remnant**: a named echo with a horn and a title, remembered across the whole world when it falls.
- **The Sovereigns now.** The seven did not die. They hid their Fragments with their **Champions** and wait,
  half-mad, to be called by anyone foolish enough to gather six Fragments and a token. A sigil wakes them.
- **The Old Powers.** Older than the Crown: the **Wither**, the **Ender Dragon**, the **Warden**, the **Elder
  Guardian**. They never served the Sovereigns. Each keeps a relic of the world before.
- **Runes.** When anything dies, its light leaves as runes. The Ashborn can hold runes, spend them at a shrine to
  grow, and lose them on death. They wait where you fell, guarded by your Echo.

## The player: an Ashborn

You rose from ash with no name. Your **origin** is the life you half-remember (Runeborn's origins: Knight-Errant
to Ashborn, the weakest start). You can't die for good, which is exactly why the Remnants hunt you: an Ashborn's
light is the brightest thing left in the world.

## Places

- **Last Light**: the village where you light your first shrine. It becomes your home as the Keeper, the Smith and
  the Huntmaster arrive one by one.
- **The Labyrinths**: prisons the Sovereigns built for things they could not kill (Much More Dungeons).
- **The Deep**: the dark under the world, where the Warden keeps the oldest relic.
- **The Ashlands**: the Nether, where Morgrath's ash still falls.
- **The Eclipsed Realm**: a dimension under a black sun, seven biomes, three arenas behind fog gates, and the
  throne.

### The ending

When the Hollow Sovereign falls, the light he drank returns. Every player sees the title **FIRST LIGHT**, the Eclipse
bar fills, and the Keeper's last line: *"Rest, Ashborn. The sun remembers you."* Then one more message, from nowhere:
*"Seven crowns, one hollow. But who broke the sun?"*: the Season 2 hook.

## What makes this pack its own

1. **Your death leaves a Remnant.** Die and an **Echo** of you rises where you fell: a named elite ("Echo of
   <you>") guarding your lost runes. Kill your own Echo to take them back. Die again first, and the old Echo fades
   with your runes. (KubeJS + RPG Mobs' spawn command + Runeborn's rune drop.)
2. **The Long Eclipse bar.** A world-wide progress bar: *"The Long Eclipse: 0 / 8 embers rekindled."* Each Sovereign's
   first defeat on a world rekindles an ember for everyone: a title, a sound, and a permanent **Emberblessed** bonus
   heart for every player. Co-op groups share the climb.
3. **Hunter's Moon nights.** On a red moon the Remnants hunt: more elites, named Remnants with horns, better spoils.
   The pack's quests teach you to survive them, then to farm them.
4. **The Ashen Contracts.** A repeatable bounty board (FTB Quests) with rank-themed rewards, so the mid-game never
   runs dry.
5. **A home that grows.** Last Light gains faces as you progress: each act's quest rewards bring a new named NPC to
   your shrine.
6. **One soulslike rule set.** Runes, shrines, flasks, stamina and the dodge come from Runeborn; levels come from
   RPG Stats; mob ranks from RPG Mobs. Overlapping systems are switched off so every number means one thing.

## Tone and wording rules

- Dark fantasy, short sentences, dry humour from the Ashen Company. Never parody, never meme in quest text.
- Second person in quests ("you"), the Keeper speaks in italics.
- "Soulslike" for the fights; never name another game, studio or franchise.
- Use the mods' own names for things (runes, Ember Shrine, Valor, Sovereign, Remnant, Hunter's Moon) so players can
  look them up.
