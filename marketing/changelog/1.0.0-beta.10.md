## 1.0.0-beta.10: Fixes from the Ashborn

### Fixed

- **Two Act I quests could not be finished.**
  - *Into the Dark Places* only counted one kind of dungeon. Any dungeon now counts: spawner dungeons, the
    catacombs, crypts, frozen vaults, sunken tombs and the rest.
  - *Champion's Bane* could never complete. Killing a **champion** (a purple ring and a boss bar of its own) now
    completes it, and every champion drops a **Sovereign Fragment**, as the story always promised.
- **Server crash from a Truffler** (Spore & Stem 2.0.1). A world with that Truffler nearby could not load; it loads
  again after updating.
- **Item duplication with OmniVault.** Breaking a Storage Terminal (or another network device) while it was
  connected to a Nexus Core dropped the item and left the block in place. Fixed in OmniVault 2.0.3.
- **A backpack put inside itself disappeared** with everything in it. Backpacks now refuse backpacks of their own
  type (Core Utilities 2.0.3).

### Updated

Core Utilities 2.0.3, OmniVault 2.0.3, Spore & Stem 2.0.1 and RPG Stats 5.1.2.

Worlds from beta 9 load unchanged. Players stuck on the two quests: enter any dungeon and kill any champion after
updating.
