## 1.0.0-beta.12: Champion's Bane, for real

### Fixed

- **Champion's Bane (Act I) would not complete for many players, and champions often dropped no Fragment.**
  Beta 10 only counted a champion when your own strike was the killing blow. A champion that burned in the
  daylight, bled out, fell, or was finished by a sellsword still showed the *Champion's Bane* popup, but the quest
  stayed open and no **Sovereign Fragment** dropped.
  - The quest now completes on that same popup. If you already had it, the quest ticks the next time you log in:
    no new kill needed.
  - Every champion you wound and bring down drops its Fragment, however it dies. A **Remnant** (the rank above a
    champion) counts as one too.
- **The Fields of Last Light** (The Ash Garden) waited on *Let It Pour*, which was marked optional. It is a normal
  quest now, so the chapter no longer hides a required step behind an optional one.
- **See-through blocks beside OmniVault's Storage and Crafting Terminals**, and bus cables that ended in mid-air
  next to an Import, Export or Storage Bus (OmniVault 2.0.4).
- **A backpack's jukebox kept playing after you died** (Sophisticated Core 1.5.32).

### Changed

- **The Gingerbread House is now a whole winter's worth of the Coven's baking** (The Gingerbread House 2.0.0): the
  house is rebuilt from gingerbread, icing and candy blocks and turns up more often, and snowy lands gain decorated
  trees, presents, reindeer, gingerbread men and Krampus. *The Wayfarer*'s Gingerbread House quest is unchanged.
  New things only appear in land you have not explored yet.
