## 1.0.0-beta.15: The Codex Opens

### Fixed

- **The Ashen Codex said "Invalid book" and wouldn't open.** The guide book from the first Prologue quest now opens
  with all of its pages: the Sovereigns, the world, the dead, the people of Last Light and the old powers. If you
  already have the broken copy in your inventory, it's swapped for a working one the next time you log in.

No mods changed since beta 14. Worlds from beta 14 load unchanged, and quest progress is kept.
