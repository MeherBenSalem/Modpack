## 1.0.0-beta.6: The Ember Forge

### The Ember Forge: a new progression for the whole pack

Progress is earned now, not crafted around. Six alloys follow the six parts of the story, and almost everything
worth building asks for the one its act belongs to. Plan on **80+ hours** for a first run.

| Tier | Opens after | Alloy | Made with |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | your first Ember Shrine | **Ashen Alloy** | a Create mixer: iron, andesite alloy, a Faded Rune |
| II | the Dusk Rider | **Duskforged Ingot** | the Seal of Dusk, Create golden sheets, a Remnant Shard, a Tempering Stone II |
| III | Varghast and Sylvara | **Moonsilver Ingot** | the Seal of the Hunt, diamonds, amethyst, a Lustrous Stone I, blasted |
| IV | Kaelthar, Ysolde and the Warden | **Deepsteel Ingot** | the Seal of the Deep, a Reinforced Echo Shard, prismarine, haunted through soul fire |
| V | Morgrath, Vaelorn, the Wither and the Dragon | **Sunforged Ingot** | the Seal of Ash, netherite, blaze powder, mixed over a heated blaze burner |
| VI | the Eclipsed Realm | **Eclipse Core** | Create mechanical crafting: Sunforged, Grave Iron, Royal Soul Fragments, an Eclipse Shard |

- **Seals** are bound once from the Sovereigns' relics (plus a dish from the Hearth's kitchens) and stay in the
  crafting grid. A relic buys its armament *or* a seal; call the Sovereign again with its sigil to have both.
- **The story locks itself.** Each Sovereign's sigil needs the seal of the act before (the Dusk Rider's needs Ashen
  Alloy), and the Eclipse Shard needs Sunforged and the Seal of Ash. No more skipping to the Realm.
- **New chapter: The Ember Forge** (after the Prologue) walks the six tiers.

### Two storage mods, one ladder

- Every Sophisticated Backpacks, Sophisticated Storage and OmniVault recipe is tiered on the alloys: copper is Create
  copper sheets, iron is Ashen Alloy, gold Duskforged, diamond Moonsilver, netherite Deepsteel; storage blocks count
  one tier higher, nether stars become Eclipse Cores.
- The two mods need each other: a backpack starts as a Supplementaries sack, OmniVault drawers are built on
  Sophisticated barrels, the OmniVault Logic Processor runs the Sophisticated Controller and every advanced upgrade,
  the Storage Hub is built around a Sophisticated Controller, the Crafting Terminal needs a backpack Crafting Upgrade,
  the Wireless Terminal needs a Storage Link.
- No Nether quartz yet? Mix amethyst, redstone and a Remnant Shard in a Create basin for Omnium dust.
- **New chapter: The Vaults** (Hearth and Harvest) walks the ladder; Last Light keeps the house, sacks, wands and the
  village.

### Also tied to the Forge

- **Create is required** now (the Clockwork Path's mixer, press, fan, burner and crafters).
- Diamond armour and tools take a Duskforged Ingot.
- Building wands follow the tiers (iron Ashen, diamond Moonsilver, netherite Deepsteel, infinity an Eclipse Core).
- Nature's Compass needs Ashen Alloy, the Explorer's Compass Duskforged.

### Quality of life

- The GUI scale starts at **2** instead of Auto on a new install.

### Upgrading a beta.5 world

Recipes change the moment you update; items you already made stay. The storage quests moved from Last Light to The
Vaults, so tick them again there (the quest book does it for items you already hold).
