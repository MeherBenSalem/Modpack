## 1.0.0-beta.7: Chronicles of the Ash

### Fixed from your reports

- **The gap before the first boss.** The fog gate in the Threshold had floor only in its middle block; one step to
  the side dropped you into the void with everything you carried. It is solid now, and the Threshold catches
  anyone who falls off the ruin and puts them back on the path, with nothing lost.
- **"How do I get my things back?"** After every death, chat tells you where you fell and how to recover both
  things you lost: your **items** wait in your **corpse** (right-click it), your **runes** in the **bloodstain**
  beside it, which your **Echo** guards (kill it, then step in). **Ctrl+U** opens the list of your last deaths (it
  shared U with the Fellowship roster before, so it never opened). The prologue's death quests explain the same.
- **Creepers everywhere.** Genetically Modified Monsters became **Spore & Stem**, so its seven creeper variants and
  their extra spawns are gone. Vanilla creepers are back to vanilla numbers.

### RPG Stats gates

Gear now asks for the attribute that fits it, gently and along the acts: weapons and tools **Attack Power**, armour
**Protection**, guns and bows **Attack Speed**, anything that flies **Agility**, compasses **Exploration**,
gauntlets and the heaviest hammers **Fortitude**. Modded ores follow diamond's level gate. Level-ups now pay runes,
tempering stones and lustrous stones instead of free diamonds and diamond armour.

### Balance

- OmniVault storage matrices use plain gold and diamonds again (the Forge's alloys compounded into thousands of
  ingots by 16M); Logic Processors craft two at a time. The processors keep the tier gate.
- Locked loot bags pay runes, stones, whetstones and diamonds instead of maxed netherite.
- The Scroll of Rebirth can be crafted again (its recipe never loaded on 1.21.1).
- The Farming Crate quest now waits for Duskforged (its diamond hoe needs one).
