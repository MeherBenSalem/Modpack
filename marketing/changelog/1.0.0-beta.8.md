## 1.0.0-beta.8: Fixes from your reports

Every NightBeam mod in the pack is updated to its newest version, with the fixes from your CurseForge comments and
Discord tickets.

### Fixed

- **Fog gates and Grace Embers had missing textures** (purple and black) in the Eclipsed Realm. Fixed in Remnant
  Bosses 3.1.1, so the pack no longer needs its own patch for them.
- **Ghost blocks and missing gear requirements in tooltips.** One malformed RPG Stats gate entry could break every
  block break, item use and attack. RPG Stats 5.1.1 skips bad entries, and every reward listed for a level is
  given now (runes, tempering stones and lustrous stones on the same level), including when you gain several
  levels at once.
- **Dungeon spawners never spawned anything.** The room lights kept 36 of Much More Dungeons' 45 spawners dark
  enough to never fire. They spawn whatever the light level now (dungeons generated after updating).
- **Medium and Big Backpack crash** (slot 27) in Core Utilities.
- **Server freeze when a Meridian Archive site loaded** (Roguelite Dungeons).
- **World-load crash with mods that throw while rolling loot** in Mob LootBags.
- Remnants | Fellowship and Remnants | Skills and Powers no longer ship a file with `|` in its name, which failed
  to unpack on Windows and Android.

### New

- **Rift Stabilizer** (Chronofall 2.1): no natural Chrono Rift opens within 32 blocks of it. Craft it in the
  Fabricator.
- RE:Constructed Wands can place barriers, light blocks and structure voids in creative mode again.

### Updated

Remnant Bosses 3.1.1, RPG Stats 5.1.1, Chronofall 2.1.0, RPG Mobs 3.1.2, Skills and Powers 2.0.2, OmniVault 2.0.2,
Core Utilities 2.0.1, Mob LootBags 2.0.1, Much More Dungeons 2.0.1, RE:Constructed Wands 2.0.1,
Remnants | Fellowship 2.0.1, Remnants Minimap 2.0.1, Roguelite Dungeons 1.0.1 and The Witch Hut 2.0.0.

The server pack's config files are regenerated. Worlds from beta 7 load unchanged.
