## 1.0.0-beta.9: The Keeper's voice

### New: the Keeper speaks

- **A new voice for the Keeper.** All of the Keeper's lines are re-recorded by a real storyteller's voice instead of
  the placeholder: every act's tale, every Sovereign's fall, the Threshold and the epilogue.
- **An opening tale.** On your first join, after you choose your origin, the Keeper tells the story of the Long
  Eclipse over a black screen before you wake in the Crypt: the broken sun, the eighth who drank the light, the
  Remnants, and you. About 80 seconds; sneak to skip it.
- **The Keeper knows the land.** The first time you walk into each of the 30 named lands (the Ashen Fields, the
  Hollow Wood, the Deep, the Ashlands, the Last Shore and the rest), the Keeper has a word to say about it.
  Turn the Keeper down with the *Voice/Speech* volume slider.

### Fixed

- **Crash when putting an enchanted item in a backpack.** Moving an enchanted item or book into a Small, Medium or
  Big Backpack crashed the game. Fixed in Core Utilities 2.0.2. Items already in a backpack are kept.

### Balance

- **The Warden is no longer a loot and level fountain.** Ancient City shriekers summon a new Warden whenever they
  are triggered, and every kill paid 3 Epic-or-better loot bags and a boss's worth of RPG levels, so farming it took
  players from level 25 to 170. The vanilla Warden now drops loot bags like any other strong mob and pays 100 VP
  instead of 500. The pack's own bosses, the Wither, the Ender Dragon and the Elder Guardian are unchanged.

### Tip: lost a lens material?

Each world has three Fractured Shrines, 192 to 640 blocks from world spawn, and every one has its own Fracture Coffer
with a full set of Cracked Yesterglass Lens materials (3 Chronicle Shards, 2 Yesterglass, 1 Lenswork Crystal). If a
creeper took yours, the next shrine has another set.
