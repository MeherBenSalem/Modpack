# Credits and licences: the beta 9 reel

## Footage

Every frame is real gameplay of **Remnants: Ashen Legacy** 1.0.0-beta.9, recorded for this reel on the pack's own
client and server pack (`source/capture/`). No AI-generated images or video, no upscaling, no mock interface: the
HUD, boss bars, level and rank plates, title cards and area names on screen are the pack's own.

## Voice

The Keeper of Last Light, from the pack's own in-game recordings in `src/voice/raw/` (made with ElevenLabs for
beta 9: voice "Arkadius - Old Storyteller", `O4uxPTtvgxrA0QCLHI1J`, model `eleven_v4`). The reel reuses ten phrases
from nine existing takes (`SCRIPT.md`); no new lines were generated for it, so it used **0 ElevenLabs characters**.
