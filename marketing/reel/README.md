## How it was made

- **Footage.** Every shot is the real pack: the server pack from `tools/build_pack.py --export`, unpacked into a
  scratch folder with RCON on, and the real client installed by `tools/test_client.py`'s installer
  (`source/capture/client_prep.py`), joined to it. Both ran on a clock slowed 30× with libfaketime
  (`source/capture/game.sh`); the 1920×1920 window on an Xvfb display was grabbed at 8 fps (`source/capture/cap.sh`),
  which keeps about 90–120 renders for every second of game time. `source/capture/shots.py` stages and drives every
  shot: RCON for the stage (time of day, kit, ranked spawns with RPG Mobs' own `remnants spawn`, `/threshold send`
  for the wake and Ser Odric's fog gate, the Dusk Rider from its Sigil, the Echo from a real death), and real key
  presses and mouse moves for the player (Shoulder Surfing's camera, Better Combat swings, Runeborn's dodge roll and
  Ember Flask). `source/capture/intro.py` records the opening tale as a brand-new player sees it.
- **Smooth playback, no lag.** `source/prep.py` indexes each capture's unique renders and their game times;
  `source/compose.py` asks for a game time per 60 fps output frame and averages the renders inside half a frame
  interval (a 180° shutter), cross-fading between neighbours. Nothing plays slower than real time, no frame is
  repeated. A light per-frame film grain keeps even held black and title frames distinct.
- **Framing.** The portrait and landscape cuts are crops of the same graded 1920×1920 frame. Shots with the pack's
  HUD on use windows that keep only whole HUD elements (boss bar, plates, hotbar) and never a cut-off fragment.
- **Voice.** Only the pack's own beta 9 recordings of the Keeper (ElevenLabs, "Arkadius - Old Storyteller",
  `eleven_v4`), cut at measured silences by `source/build_vo.py` from `source/voice/script.json`. Nothing new was
  generated, so the reel cost no ElevenLabs credits. Every cut was transcribed with faster-whisper and matched the
  script word for word (`source/wordtimes.py`), and `source/verify.py` checks the finished mix the same way.
  The voice gets only the channel narration chain; music and effects are sidechain-ducked under it
  (`source/final.py`).
- **Sound.** Mixkit recordings (`source/fetch_sfx.sh`), each one's measured hit placed on its frame
  (`source/audio.py`, cues in `source/src_times.json`): a church bell, trailer impacts and whooshes on the cuts,
  swords on armour on the beats, Ser Odric's Ash Slam on the Keeper's word "lands".
- **Music.** "Epical Drums 03" (Mixkit), one continuous excerpt from 2:06.04 placed at 4.0 s: its breakdown sits
  under the fog gate, its drop lands on the first clash with Ser Odric (25.0 s) and its last hit on the title (49.0 s).

## Rebuild

Scratch folder `$REEL` (default `/home/user/reel`): raw captures, voice cuts, renders. Then:

1. `python3 tools/build_pack.py --export`; unpack the server pack into `$REEL/server` (RCON on, see
   `source/capture/game.sh`); `source/capture/client_prep.py`; `game.sh server`, `game.sh client` (Xvfb `:2`, 1920×1920).
2. `source/capture/shots.py gear`, then the shots named in `source/src_times.json`; `source/capture/intro.py`.
3. `source/prep.py <clip>...` and `source/sheet.py <clip>` to read event times.
4. `source/wordtimes.py $REEL/wordtimes.json src/voice/raw/<takes>.mp3`, `source/build_vo.py`, `source/plan.py`.
5. `source/fetch_sfx.sh`, `source/audio.py`, `source/compose.py`, `source/final.py`, then
   `source/verify.py $REEL/out/*.mp4`.

Never run `compose.py` while the game is capturing: the client, the server and the renderer together exceed the
container's memory.
